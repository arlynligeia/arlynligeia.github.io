Welcome to the universe of Arlyn Ligeia…

Here you will find the works of Arlyn Ligeia, as well as fragments of its thoughts, obsessions, questions, and transformations.

BIOGRAPHY

X

LUCA DESCENDANT…

Ligeia, also known as Arlyn Ligeia, is a Peruvian multidisciplinary artist working across film, music, fashion, and art.

She grew up in Peru, far from the film, music, and fashion industries. Film and music became her natural language and, having no access to any of those worlds, she learned to build her own.

She studied Psychology at university and complemented her education with studies in Audiovisual Communication and Fashion Management and Design. She currently holds a B.S. in Psychology and a diploma in Fashion Management and Design.

In 2021, she directed her first audiovisual work, a music video for an independent band, marking the beginning of her career as a director. In 2024, she wrote, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited her first short film, taking on every role in the production from beginning to end.

That same year, she was admitted to the Master's Degree in Performing Arts at the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru. However, she decided to postpone this academic path in order to dedicate herself fully to building her own body of work.

After completing a music video and a short film, she is currently developing her first fashion collection and a mid-length film, while simultaneously working on her debut album and her first feature film.

Ligeia's artistic trajectory is a conscious pursuit of inner authenticity. She embraces her femininity alongside her interests and passions, without separating them. In contrast to neutrality, she deliberately chooses visual and sensory stimulation as a form of fidelity to herself.

Ligeia defines her art as authentic and visceral, a space where she freely explores female desire. She identifies with the femme fatale archetype and with femininity understood as a deliberate force; a reflection of herself that becomes a recurring theme in her work.

She refuses to be confined by labels.

True art does not ask for permission.

BE MY PATRON

X

Dear future patron,

In the courts of the Renaissance, artists presented themselves before their protectors to be sustained by them. Art without patronage rarely reached its fullness, and although centuries have passed, the principle remains intact. When Leonardo da Vinci wrote to Ludovico Sforza, he presented a precise catalogue of what he was capable of conceiving, building, and defending, inviting the duke to claim the privilege of being the first to commission those works. That same spirit guides this letter.

I therefore extend an open invitation to those who wish to support my journey as an artist.

Below, I detail ten capabilities that define my work:

1. I build a universe where film, music, fashion, jewelry, and technology converge under a single vision: creating authentic art without reducing myself to a single discipline.

2. Each project is born from interdisciplinary rigor. Psychology, physics, and biology intertwine to generate narratives that are then translated into film, sound, silhouettes, and signature pieces.

3. I have multidisciplinary training: a degree in Psychology, studies in Audiovisual Communication, and a diploma in Fashion Management and Design. I complement this foundation with self-taught knowledge in programming (HTML, CSS, and notions of Java).

4. I write any story with ease and transform it into a film script, concept album, song lyrics, fashion collection, jewelry, painting, or poetry. I control the narrative from its origin to its final form.

5. I direct and plan with precision. I know scriptwriting, directing, and detailed set design.

6. I design fashion and jewelry. I create figurines, handle pattern-making notions, and create costume jewelry and fine jewelry pieces. My work always starts from a coherent and recognizable aesthetic vision.

7. I master everyday and intense makeup, and I create wound and blood effects with basic characterization techniques (latex, paint, and textures), suitable for photography, screen, and runway.

8. I have real experience in beauty and fashion product sales. I reject the myth that authentic art must be poor or anti-establishment. I believe, on the contrary, that true art is born from within, from one's own experiences, and can dialogue with luxury and the market without losing its integrity.

9. I maintain absolute control over my work. Each project is conceived, directed, and executed under a single vision, without intermediaries that dilute it. I am building a distinctive aesthetic situated between science, artificial aesthetics, luxury, and transhumanist thought.

10. I have a clear path: the launch of my fashion collection accompanied by a medium-length film in 2027, along with a feature film and a concept album currently in development. And when something is necessary and I don't yet know it, I learn it.

With gratitude,

Arlyn Ligeia
