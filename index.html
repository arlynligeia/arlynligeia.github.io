Ladies and gentlemen,

welcome to the universe of ARLYN LIGEIA…

A space where the cosmic, the technological, and the sensory converge; where the past and the future meet in the present; where emotion and reason coexist; and where art becomes a way of exploring that which we do not yet understand.

The Arlyn Ligeia universe explores the transformation of the human condition and the possibilities of what could exist beyond its biological limits. Its work moves through transhumanism and posthumanism, exploring the boundary between the human and the artificial, the biological and the designed, the mortal and that which aspires to transcend death.

Its imaginary is nourished by the exploration of the cosmos, spacetime, the mechanisms of life, the mind, cognition, consciousness, and the possibilities opened by technology and the evolution of human capabilities.

Depth psychology, biology, physics, astrophysics, and emerging technologies form part of this creative territory. As fields from which questions arise about the origin, nature, limits, and future of existence.

Visually, this universe draws inspiration from vaporwave, cyberglam, space glam, retrofuturism, 1990s pop culture, Pop Art, and Art Deco.

The conceptual framework of Arlyn Ligeia lies in the exploration of transhumanism, posthumanism, science, technology, and the transformation of the human condition.

For Ligeia, art is a language that originates within and projects itself outward. Therefore, a film, a song, a garment, an accessory, a jewel, an image, a painting, or a verse can form part of the same exploration. Each work constitutes a fragment of a larger universe, a way of investigating, imagining, feeling, and materializing that which does not yet exist.

Here you will find the works of Arlyn Ligeia, as well as fragments of its thoughts, obsessions, questions, and transformations.

BEYOND THE BIOLOGICAL BOUNDARY…
Arlyn Ligeia

BIOGRAPHY

X

LUCA DESCENDANT…

Ligeia, also known as Arlyn Ligeia, is a Peruvian multidisciplinary artist working across film, music, fashion, and art.

She grew up in Peru, far from the film, music, and fashion industries. Film and music became her natural language and, having no access to any of those worlds, she learned to build her own.

She studied Psychology at university and complemented her education with studies in Audiovisual Communication and Fashion Management and Design. She currently holds a B.S. in Psychology and a diploma in Fashion Management and Design.

In 2021, she directed her first audiovisual work, a music video for an independent band, marking the beginning of her career as a director. In 2024, she wrote, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited her first short film, taking on every role in the production from beginning to end.

That same year, she was admitted to the Master's Degree in Performing Arts at the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru. However, she decided to postpone this academic path in order to dedicate herself fully to building her own body of work.

After completing a music video and a short film, she is currently developing her first fashion collection and a mid-length film, while simultaneously working on her debut album and her first feature film.

Ligeia's artistic trajectory is a conscious pursuit of inner authenticity. She embraces her femininity alongside her interests and passions, without separating them. In contrast to neutrality, she deliberately chooses visual and sensory stimulation as a form of fidelity to herself.

Ligeia defines her art as authentic and visceral, a space where she freely explores female desire. She identifies with the femme fatale archetype and with femininity understood as a deliberate force; a reflection of herself that becomes a recurring theme in her work.

She refuses to be confined by labels.

True art does not ask for permission.

BE MY PATRON

Dear Future Patron,

In the courts of the Renaissance, artists presented themselves to their patrons. When Leonardo da Vinci wrote to Ludovico Sforza, he offered a catalogue of what he could build, defend, and create, and invited the ruler of Milan to claim the privilege of commissioning it first. That same principle guides this letter.

I am building a universe where cinema, music, and fashion converge under a single law: that science and beauty are not opposites. This is a body of work already in motion, extending an invitation to those who wish to shape its next chapter.

What I bring to this alliance:

- A universe built on scientific rigor; physics, biology, and psychology translated into cinema, sound, and silhouette.

- A distinctive aesthetic language, already defined, already recognizable, positioned at the intersection of luxury, science, and the transhuman.

- Absolute authorial control; every project is written, directed, and executed by a single vision, with no intermediaries to dilute it.

- An unusual multidisciplinary formation; psychology, audiovisual communication, and fashion design, converted into a single creative language.

- A clear roadmap; a fashion collection and its medium-length film debuting before the end of the year, with an album and a feature film already in development.

What patronage of this work looks like:

- Ongoing Patronage: A lasting alliance for those who wish to accompany this universe as it unfolds. Early access to unreleased work, insight into the creative process, and a permanent place among its first witnesses.

- Full Project Patronage: For those who wish to attach their name to a defining chapter; full backing of a film, a collection, or a body of work, with shared credit and direct involvement in how it reaches the world. I invite you to reach out directly, and together we will design that collaboration.

To align with this work is what patronage has always been; recognition, granted early, of what will later be undeniable.

With gratitude,
Arlyn Ligeia

Arlyn Ligeia is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information that you provide to us directly:

- Contact information: your name and email address, used to manage contact forms or process purchases.

- Shipping and billing information: identification document and addresses necessary for product delivery.

- Payment information: your credit card details or other payment data, which are securely processed through stores such as Ko-fi and external payment providers such as PayPal and Stripe.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

- Order processing: to manage your purchases and ship your products.

- Communication: to send you notifications about your order, respond to your inquiries, or provide you with updates.

- Marketing: to send you newsletters or product information (only if you have given your consent).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your data with trusted third parties who help us operate our business, such as the sales platform, shipping companies, and payment providers, so that they can carry out their respective functions.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request:

- Access to, correction of, or updates to your personal information.

- To unsubscribe from our email list.

- The deletion of your personal data, provided we are not legally or financially obligated to retain it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: arlynligeiapp@gmail.com

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the new effective date. We recommend that you review this policy periodically.
