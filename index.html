ABOUT ARLYN LIGEIA

Ligeia, also known as Arlyn Ligeia, is an independent and multidisciplinary Peruvian artist focused on film, music, fashion, and art. Her creative universe fuses the gothic, the cosmic, and futurism, wrapped in a unique aesthetic.

Ligeia grew up in Moquegua, Peru. During her time there, her refuge was always film and music. She studied Psychology at university and, as complementary training, Audiovisual Communication and Fashion Design and Management. Currently she holds a Bachelor of Science in Psychology and a diploma in Fashion Design and Management.

In 2021, Ligeia directed her first audiovisual work, a music video for an independent band, which marked the beginning of her career as a director. In 2024, despite facing a severe economic crisis, she improvised with what she had at hand and managed to create her first short film. She wrote, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited it herself. That same year, Ligeia applied and was admitted to the Master's in Performing Arts at the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru; however, she could not continue her studies due to her financial crisis.

As a result, and after completing a music video and a short film, Ligeia is currently producing her first feature film (written, produced and eventually directed, filmed, starring and edited) entirely by her. Simultaneously, she is working on her debut album and her first fashion collection.

Ligeia draws inspiration from the gothic, the cosmos and technology, as well as from vaporwave, retrofuturistic aesthetics, liminal space and pop culture.

Ligeia's artistic trajectory is a conscious search for inner authenticity. She embraces her femininity along with her interests and passions, without rigidly separating them. Thus, in the face of neutrality and prejudices, Ligeia firmly chooses visual and sensory stimulation as an act of creative honesty and fidelity to herself.

Ligeia defines her art as authentic and visceral, a space where she freely explores female sexual desire. She admires the archetype of the femme fatale and exaggerated femininity, a recurring theme in her work. She refuses to be pigeonholed and opposes any prejudice, convinced that true art arises from authenticity and the fusion of diverse stimuli.

FEATURE FILM

FEATURE FILM

ALONE

UPCOMING

Written, directed, shot, starred in, and edited by Arlyn Ligeia

SYNOPSIS

A horror content creator puts a retrocausal protocol to the test. What seemed like a simple experiment turns sinister when the rules of reality begin to break around her.

CURRENTLY IN PRE-PRODUCTION

ALONE is Arlyn Ligeia's first feature film. This is a very personal project for Ligeia, born from adversity and a profound exploration of solitude during one of the most financially challenging periods of her life.

Yet, rather than giving up, Ligeia found a way to turn pain into art. This film was written by Ligeia and will be directed, shot, starred in, and edited by her.

This feature film will mark Ligeia's debut in the world of cinema. A science fiction story blending psychological and cosmic horror with a deeply dreamlike aesthetic.

Entirely self-produced, the film is a bold statement of artistic independence, shaped by her background in psychology.

This film is a love letter to artistic survival, to the loneliness that fuels creation, and to the relentless pursuit of one's deepest dreams, even in the darkest of times.

ALONE is currently in the pre-production stage.

OCTOBER

Written, produced, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited by Arlyn Ligeia

SYNOPSIS

A woman trapped in routine finds a key and a warning. The decision she makes changes the course of her life.

CURRENTLY IN PRODUCTION

SS COLLECTION

SS COLLECTION

SS27 ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57

ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57 is a ready-to-wear collection for spring/summer 2027 that was born as an extension of a medium-length film of the same name. Both projects were conceived in parallel as a critique of the growing social homogenization and the weight of repression on the contemporary individual.

The project emerged in February 2025, when the chromatic palette that would guide the entire proposal was defined. Three months later, in May, the first sketches and structures of the collection began to be developed. However, economic setbacks forced both the collection and the film to be postponed until October 2026, a date strategically chosen as the closing highlight for the season.

Aesthetically, the collection bets on an explosion of color and a silhouette with a strong feminine character, inspired by the archetype of the femme fatale, powerful, enveloping, and theatrical.

BE MY PATRON

Dear future Patron,

During the Renaissance, great patrons like Ludovico Sforza and the Medici family funded and protected artists. Thanks to them, artists like Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Botticelli were able to devote themselves to creating works that define our culture today. Their funding and support allowed the beauty of art and innovation to transcend their time.

Today, in a world often dominated by the immediate, the commercial, and the fleeting, I firmly believe that patronage is more necessary than ever. It's not just about giving for the sake of giving, but about building an alliance between artist and patron and viceversa, in order to transcend time.

By becoming my patron, you fund a process of creativity, research, and constant study. Your direct support:
• Funds my training in new disciplines that enrich my artistic vision.
• Covers development, production, and presentation costs.
• Gives me the most valuable resource of all, time, to delve deeply into each work, free from commercial constraints.

To make this journey possible, you can choose from different forms of support, depending on each patron's vision and scope:
• Remote Patronage (Ongoing Support): Through my digital platforms, you can make one-time or recurring contributions. This support sustains my daily work and covers essential materials, technology, and the upkeep of my studio space.
• Comprehensive Project Patronage (Direct, Personalized Support): If your wish is to drive the full production of a specific large-scale work, fund artistic residencies and research travel, or take on long-term sponsorship of my career and multiple collections over the years. I invite you to get in touch with me directly. Together, we can design a tailored collaboration to bring these ideas to international stages.

Being my patron in the 21st century is an act of faith in an authentic artistic path, one that makes it possible for personal, daring projects to see the light of day, projects that would otherwise remain only ideas. Together, we can prove that essential art still comes from human connection and conscious support, not from algorithms.

With deep gratitude,

Arlyn Ligeia
