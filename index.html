UNIVERSE

Ladies and Gentlemen,

Welcome to my Universe...

This is a space where the cosmic, the technological, and the vaporwave coexist; where past and future merge into the present; where emotion and reason unite into a single feeling; and where art and science intertwine with a shared purpose, the expression and curiosity that arise from within me to manifest as art.

My work moves through transhumanism, tracing the edge between the human and the artificial, the biological and the designed, the mortal and what comes after, approached without judgment, only precision. That same curiosity draws me toward the sciences that inhabit my art; psychology, physics, and biology, three disciplines through which I explore the depths of the human mind, the immensity of space and time, and the evolution of life, from what we have been to what we are becoming.

To me, art is a language of the soul, born from within and moving outward.

Every project, whether a film, a song, a garment, an accessory, a piece of jewelry, a painting, or a verse, is born from a need to explore, feel, and transmit.

Here you will find my works, as well as fragments of my thoughts, my obsessions, my questions, and my transformations.

Arlyn Ligeia

ABOUT ARLYN LIGEIA

LUCA DESCENDANT…

Ligeia, also known as Arlyn Ligeia, is a multidisciplinary Peruvian artist working in film, music, fashion, and art.

She grew up in Moquegua, Peru, far from any film, music, or fashion industry. Film and music became her natural language, and without access to either world, she learned to build her own. She studied Psychology at university and, as complementary training, Audiovisual Communication and Fashion Design and Management. She currently holds a Bachelor's Degree in Psychology and a diploma in Fashion Design and Management.

In 2021, she directed her first audiovisual work, a music video for an independent band, marking the beginning of her career as a director. In 2024, she wrote, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited her first short film, taking on every role of the production from start to finish. That same year, she applied to and was admitted into the Master's Program in Performing Arts at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú. She chose to postpone this achievement to fully commit to building her own body of work.

After completing a music video and a short film, she is currently producing her first fashion collection alongside a medium-length film, while simultaneously working on her debut album and her first feature film.

Ligeia's artistic journey is a conscious search for inner authenticity. She embraces her femininity alongside her interests and passions, without separating them. In the face of neutrality, she firmly chooses visual and sensory stimulation as an act of fidelity to herself.

Ligeia defines her art as authentic and visceral, a space where she freely explores female desire. She identifies with the archetype of the femme fatale and femininity as a deliberate force, a reflection of herself that becomes a recurring theme in her work. She rejects being pigeonholed. True art asks no permission.

FEATURE FILM

FEATURE FILM

ALONE

COMING SOON

Written, produced, directed, starred in, filmed, and edited by Arlyn Ligeia

SYNOPSIS

A horror content creator puts a retrocausal protocol to the test. What seemed like a simple experiment turns sinister as the rules of reality begin to break down around her.

CURRENTLY IN PRE-PRODUCTION

ALONE is Ligeia's first feature film. This is a deeply personal project, born out of adversity and a profound exploration of solitude during one of the most economically difficult periods of her life.

However, instead of giving up, Ligeia found a way to transform pain into art. The screenplay has been written by Ligeia, who will also produce, direct, star in, film, and edit the film herself.

This feature film will mark Ligeia's debut in the world of cinema. A science fiction story that blends psychological and cosmic horror with a deeply dreamlike aesthetic.

ALONE is currently in the pre-production stage.

MEDIUM-LENGTH FILM

MEDIUM-LENGTH FILM

ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57

AVAILABLE IN OCTOBER

Written, produced, directed, starring, filmed, and edited by Arlyn Ligeia

SYNOPSIS

A woman trapped in routine finds a key and a warning. The choice she makes changes the course of her life.

CURRENTLY IN PRODUCTION

ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57 is a medium-length psychological surrealist drama film, conceived in parallel with the fashion collection of the same name, as a critique of an increasingly homogenized and repressed society.

The idea for this project was born in February 2025, and by May of that same year, formal development of its first elements began. However, due to financial delays, both the film's production and the collection's were postponed, with their release now set for October 2026, closing out the Spring/Summer 2027 season.

SS COLLECTION

SS COLLECTION

SS27 ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57

ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57 is a prêt-à-porter collection for Spring/Summer 2027, born as an extension of the medium-length film of the same name. Both projects were conceived in parallel as a critique of growing social homogenization and the weight of repression on the contemporary individual.

The project began in February 2025, when the color palette that would guide the entire proposal was defined. Three months later, in May, the first sketches and structural elements of the collection began to take shape. However, financial setbacks forced the postponement of both the collection and the film. The collection's launch is set for October 2026, a date strategically chosen as the season's culminating event.

Aesthetically, the collection embraces an explosion of color and a silhouette of strong feminine character, inspired by the femme fatale archetype — powerful, enveloping, and theatrical.

BE MY PATRON

Dear Future Patron,

In the courts of the Renaissance, artists presented themselves to their patrons. When Leonardo da Vinci wrote to Ludovico Sforza, he offered a catalogue of what he could build, defend, and create, and invited the ruler of Milan to claim the privilege of commissioning it first. That same principle guides this letter.

I am building a universe where cinema, music, and fashion converge under a single law: that science and beauty are not opposites. This is a body of work already in motion, extending an invitation to those who wish to shape its next chapter.

What I bring to this alliance:

- A universe built on scientific rigor; physics, biology, and psychology translated into cinema, sound, and silhouette.

- A distinctive aesthetic language, already defined, already recognizable, positioned at the intersection of luxury, science, and the transhuman.

- Absolute authorial control; every project is written, directed, and executed by a single vision, with no intermediaries to dilute it.

- An unusual multidisciplinary formation; psychology, audiovisual communication, and fashion design, converted into a single creative language.

- A clear roadmap; a fashion collection and its medium-length film debuting before the end of the year, with an album and a feature film already in development.

What patronage of this work looks like:

- Ongoing Patronage: A lasting alliance for those who wish to accompany this universe as it unfolds. Early access to unreleased work, insight into the creative process, and a permanent place among its first witnesses.

- Full Project Patronage: For those who wish to attach their name to a defining chapter; full backing of a film, a collection, or a body of work, with shared credit and direct involvement in how it reaches the world. I invite you to reach out directly, and together we will design that collaboration.

To align with this work is what patronage has always been; recognition, granted early, of what will later be undeniable.

With gratitude,

Arlyn Ligeia
