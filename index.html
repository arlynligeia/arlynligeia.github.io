ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57

A medium-length film and fashion collection of science fiction and surreal psychological drama, conceived together as a critique of an increasingly homogenized and repressed society. In this work, Ligeia explores concepts of psychoanalysis such as the repressed Jungian shadow and the search for authenticity in a world where everyone is the same.

UNIVERSE

Ladies and Gentlemen,

Welcome to my Universe...

This is a space where the gothic, the cosmic, and the futuristic coexist, where the past and the future are the present, where emotion and reason form a single feeling, and where art and science intertwine with the same purpose: the expression and curiosity born from within me to manifest as art.

For me, art is more than a medium; it is a language of the soul that is born from the inside out.

Every project, whether a feature film, a song, a garment, a jewel, a painting, a poem, or a research article, is born from a personal need to explore, feel, and connect.

Here you will not only find a collection of my works, but fragments of my thoughts, my obsessions, my questions, and my transformations.

Arlyn Ligeia
